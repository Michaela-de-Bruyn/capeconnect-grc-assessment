
# AI Chatbot Scope and System Description

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the scope and system boundary for the simulated GRC assessment of CapeConnect's proposed AI-powered customer support chatbot.

## 2. System Description

CapeConnect plans to introduce a third-party AI chatbot to assist its customer support function.

The chatbot is intended to:
- Respond to common customer questions.
- Assist with service-related enquiries.
- Collect information needed to handle support requests.
- Maintain conversation histories.
- Escalate enquiries to human support employees where necessary.

## 3. Information Potentially Processed

The assessment will consider the following information:

- Customer names
- Email addresses
- Account-related enquiries
- Customer complaints
- Support conversation histories

These are proposed data categories for the simulation, not verified records of an operational system.

## 4. Assessment Scope

The assessment will examine:

- Personal information collection and processing.
- Data storage, retention and deletion.
- User access and permissions.
- Third-party AI provider security and privacy practices.
- Use of customer conversations for AI model training.
- Employee awareness and acceptable AI use.
- Incident reporting and response.
- Relevant security and privacy controls.

## 5. System Boundary

The assessment will consider the interactions between:

- CapeConnect customers.
- The AI chatbot interface.
- CapeConnect customer support employees.
- CapeConnect IT and management.
- The external AI service provider.

The provider's internal infrastructure is outside the direct assessment boundary unless the provider supplies relevant evidence.

## 6. Key Assumptions and Unknowns

The following details require investigation:

| Area | Current position |
|---|---|
| AI provider | Not yet identified |
| Hosting location | Unknown |
| Data storage location | Unknown |
| Data retention period | Unknown |
| Model training using customer conversations | Unconfirmed |
| Employee access permissions | Requires validation |
| Data processing agreement | Evidence required |
| Security certifications | Evidence required |

## 7. Assessment Limitations

This is a simulated portfolio assessment. The system and company are fictional, and no real customer data will be used.

Unknown information will remain documented as unknown until supporting evidence becomes available.

## 8. Assessment Objective

Determine whether CapeConnect has appropriate governance, security and privacy controls to manage the risks introduced by its proposed AI chatbot.
