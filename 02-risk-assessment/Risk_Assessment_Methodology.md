
# CapeConnect Risk Assessment Methodology

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the methodology used to assess and prioritise information security, privacy and AI governance risks associated with the proposed CapeConnect AI chatbot.

## 2. Risk Assessment Approach

Each identified risk will be evaluated using two factors:

- Likelihood: How probable is the risk event?
- Impact: How serious would the consequences be if it occurred?

Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact

## 3. Likelihood Scale

| Score | Rating | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rare | Extremely unlikely to occur |
| 2 | Unlikely | Could occur, but not expected |
| 3 | Possible | Could reasonably occur |
| 4 | Likely | Expected to occur in some circumstances |
| 5 | Almost Certain | Expected to occur frequently or imminently |

## 4. Impact Scale

| Score | Rating | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Insignificant | Minimal operational impact |
| 2 | Minor | Limited disruption or minor internal consequences |
| 3 | Moderate | Noticeable operational, financial or reputational impact |
| 4 | Major | Significant disruption, financial loss or privacy consequences |
| 5 | Severe | Serious data exposure, major business disruption or significant regulatory consequences |

## 5. Risk Scoring Matrix

Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact

| Score | Risk Level |
|---|---|
| 1–4 | Low |
| 5–9 | Medium |
| 10–16 | High |
| 17–25 | Critical |

## 6. Inherent Risk

Inherent risk is the level of risk before considering the effect of existing controls.

It represents the exposure associated with the activity itself.

## 7. Residual Risk

Residual risk is the level of risk remaining after considering the design and operating effectiveness of existing controls.

A control should not automatically reduce a risk score simply because a policy or procedure exists.

Evidence must support the control assessment.

## 8. Risk Assessment Principles

- Scores must have documented reasoning.
- Risk ratings must reflect the business context.
- Unverified vulnerabilities must remain clearly identified as assumptions.
- Existing controls must be assessed before calculating residual risk.
- High and critical risks should receive appropriate management attention.
- Risk acceptance must be documented and approved by an authorised risk owner.

## 9. Limitations

This is a qualitative risk assessment methodology created for a simulated portfolio project. Scores represent assessment judgements, not statistically calculated probabilities.
