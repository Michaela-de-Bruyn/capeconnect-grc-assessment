
# CapeConnect Information Asset Register

## 1. Purpose

This register identifies the information assets associated with the proposed AI chatbot and assigns proposed ownership responsibilities.

It supports risk identification, control assessment and risk treatment.

All assets and ownership assignments are hypothetical until validated.

## 2. Information Asset Register

| ID | Information Asset | Description | Proposed Owner | Classification |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A001 | Customer Personal Information | Customer names, email addresses and account-related information | Customer Support Manager | Confidential |
| A002 | Chatbot Conversation History | Records of customer interactions with the chatbot | Customer Support Manager | Confidential |
| A003 | AI Chatbot Application | Application used to interact with customers | CTO | Internal |
| A004 | AI Provider Service | External AI service processing customer enquiries | CTO | Third-party |
| A005 | Customer Support Accounts | Employee accounts used to access conversation histories | IT Manager | Confidential |
| A006 | Access Credentials | Passwords, API keys and authentication credentials | IT Manager | Restricted |

| A009 | Data Processing Agreement | Contract governing personal information processing by the provider | Procurement / Legal | Confidential |
| A010 | Risk and Assessment Records | Risk register, findings and assessment evidence | GRC Analyst | Internal |

## 3. Asset Ownership Responsibilities

### CTO
- Oversees the chatbot technology and architecture.
- Approves technology-related decisions.
- Oversees third-party technology relationships.

### IT Manager
- Manages user access and permissions.
- Maintains technical security controls.
- Oversees system logging and monitoring.

### Customer Support Manager
- Oversees customer support processes.
- Ensures employees use the chatbot appropriately.
- Manages operational access requirements.

### Information Officer / Compliance
- Oversees privacy governance responsibilities.
- Supports POPIA compliance.
- Reviews personal information processing practices.

### Procurement / Legal
- Reviews third-party contractual arrangements.
- Supports supplier due diligence.
- Reviews data processing agreements.

### GRC Analyst
- Identifies and documents information security risks.
- Assesses relevant controls.
- Reviews evidence.
- Records findings and recommendations.
- Tracks remediation activities.

## 4. Important Assessment Considerations

The assessment must establish:

- Who owns each asset in practice?
- Who can access each asset?
- Where is each asset stored?
- How is each asset protected?
- How long is information retained?
- What happens when information is no longer required?

## 5. Register Status

Status: Initial asset identification.

Asset ownership, classification and technical details require stakeholder confirmation and supporting evidence.
