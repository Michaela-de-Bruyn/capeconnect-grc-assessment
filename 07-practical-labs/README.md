# Practical GRC Labs

This section documents hands-on Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC) exercises completed as part of the CapeConnect AI Assurance Hub portfolio.

All organisations, people, systems, evidence and findings used in these exercises are fictional and created for learning purposes. Do not upload confidential information, employer documents, customer data, credentials or real security evidence.

## How to document each lab

For every exercise, record:

1. **Objective:** What skill are you practising?
2. **Scenario:** What organisation or process are you assessing?
3. **Scope and assumptions:** What is included, excluded and unknown?
4. **Method:** What framework, criteria or scoring approach did you use?
5. **Evidence:** What information was available, missing or requested?
6. **Analysis:** What risks, control gaps or findings did you identify?
7. **Recommendation:** What action should the organisation take, who owns it and by when?
8. **Reflection:** What did you learn, and what would you improve next time?

Do not claim that a simulated exercise is a formal audit, legal opinion, certification assessment or real-world security review.
