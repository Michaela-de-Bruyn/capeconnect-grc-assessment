
# CapeConnect | AI Assurance Hub

## Project Overview

AI Assurance Hub is a simulated Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC) project focused on assessing the security, privacy and governance risks of an AI-powered customer support chatbot.

The project uses a fictional South African company, CapeConnect (Pty) Ltd, to demonstrate how organisations can identify AI-related risks, assess controls, evaluate evidence and develop practical remediation plans.

## Business Scenario

CapeConnect is a fictional B2B technology-enabled customer service company based in Cape Town, South Africa.

The company plans to introduce a third-party AI chatbot to support customer enquiries. The proposed solution may process personal information, including customer names, email addresses, account-related enquiries and support conversation histories.

This introduces information security, privacy, third-party and AI governance risks.

## Project Objectives

- Identify AI-related information security and privacy risks.
- Assess potential POPIA compliance gaps.
- Map relevant requirements to recognised frameworks.
- Evaluate control design and operating effectiveness.
- Analyse evidence and document control weaknesses.
- Develop risk treatment and remediation recommendations.
- Explore AI-assisted GRC assessment workflows.

## Frameworks and Legislation

- ISO/IEC 27001:2022
- NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF)
- Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA)
